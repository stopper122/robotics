A

The gear rule

Small gear on the motor.
Big gear on the wheel.

0times the motor spun
0times the wheel spun
?

Gears confusing? Ask Mr. Brown. Point to the gear you are unsure about."I don't know which gear goes on the motor and which goes on the wheel."

Why? The little gear has to spin around many times to turn the big gear once. That makes the wheel slow, but it pushes very hard. Slow and strong wins sumo.

Feel it: Put a small gear and a big gear on a beam so they touch. Turn the small gear with your finger while a friend tries to hold the big gear still. Then switch: turn the big gear while your friend holds the small one. Which one was harder for your friend to stop?

    If something goes wrong

    Robot drives BACKWARD
    The gears flip the wheel direction. Open Devices, click the drivetrain, and press the direction arrow to reverse it. Download again.
    Robot spins in a circle
    The left and right ports are swapped. Fix the ports in Devices.
    Drives right over the tape
    Check your number. Lower the sensor closer to the floor. Try drive velocity 70 %.
    Backs up on WHITE
    Your number is too high, or the < sign is backward. It must be "less than."
    Turns way too much or too little
    Check the gear ratio. Input = teeth on the motor gear, Output = teeth on the wheel gear. Check the wheel size (200 mm). Ask Mr. Brown if you're not sure.
    Won't connect
    Is the Brain on? Try the other Brain in the list. Try a different USB cable.

      For the teacher
      • The build is a simple two-wheel-drive design written for this guide, not an official VEX model. Part sizes are close to the VEX IQ kit; let him use the closest sizes you have.
      • The side walls are beams because the wheel shafts and motors need holes facing sideways. The top and the pusher are plates, joined to the beams with corner connectors, the way VEX builds are usually braced.
      • Part colors in the 3D picture are picked so the parts are easy to tell apart. Your kit's colors may be different.
      • For reference, the official VEX build instructions show how motors, shafts and wheels attach.
      • Gear spacing uses standard VEX IQ gears on the half-inch hole grid: 12-tooth and 36-tooth gears sit 2 holes apart; 12-tooth and 60-tooth sit 3 holes apart.
      • Quick check for understanding: ask him to point to the motor gear and the wheel gear and say which one is small.
      • The "Feel it" activity in Part A works best with loose gears before he builds.
      • If he already has a partial robot, start him at the step that matches what he has and use the earlier steps as a checklist.
      • Part C step 5 sets the gear ratio (Input 12, Output 36). With one pair of gears the wheel turns the opposite way from the motor, so if the robot drives backward, reverse the drivetrain direction in Devices (step 12 covers this).
      • You may want to do the Devices setup (steps 3 to 6) with him the first time, then have him check it against the picture.
      The 3D model is simplified to show where parts go. One grid square on the floor equals one hole space (half an inch).
